# ISTE330 - Project 1

Team Members: Alex Vasilcoiu, Daniyah Wong, Noella Abraham, Sondos Sosak, Jason Wu

> Problem Statement:
Department faculty regularly engage in research and publish their results. They are often
looking to work with other faculty and students, but it is difficult to keep track of what
each person is working on and interests to find collaborators.
Also, students are looking for faculties with similar interests to guide their capstone
projects and thesis.

> Project Description:
This is a database application to address this problem. In this database application are store abstracts of
each professor’s interests, along with student interests, so that both students and fellow faculty
could easily search the database to see if there are opportunities for collaboration.
There is also be a portal for local business, media outlets, and other members of the
public community to use the database to find speakers for current topics.
